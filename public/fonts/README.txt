Self-hosted Adobe Source Han fonts (Simplified Chinese region-specific editions).
These files are unmodified copies of Adobe's published WOFF2 variable fonts.
Both fonts cover weights 250–900 and retain their full upstream character sets.
The website uses Source Han Sans for headings/navigation and Source Han Serif
for article body text, including future articles without per-article settings.

The complete fonts total about 18 MB. CSS font-display: swap keeps text visible
while fonts download. No external font service is required at runtime.
